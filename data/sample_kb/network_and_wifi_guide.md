# NovaRetail Network and Wi-Fi Guide

## Office Wi-Fi Access
Employees connect to the NovaRetail-Corp wireless network using their company email and password. Personal devices must use the NovaRetail-Guest network, which has no access to internal systems. If the corporate network rejects your credentials, confirm your password has not expired and that your device clock is correct, then forget the network and reconnect.

## Slow or Dropped Connections
If the connection is slow, move closer to an access point, disconnect and reconnect, and check whether other users on the same floor are affected. If several users report the same issue, the Service Desk will escalate it to the Network team. Do not install personal routers or range extenders in the office.

## Guest Wi-Fi for Visitors
Visitors receive a temporary guest code from reception, valid for 24 hours. Guest access is limited to the internet and cannot reach file shares, printers, or internal applications. Employees must not share the corporate Wi-Fi credentials with visitors.

## Wired Network and Docking Stations
Conference rooms and desks provide wired Ethernet through docking stations. If a docking station does not provide network access, reseat the USB-C cable, restart the laptop, and try another port before opening a ticket with the dock's asset tag.

## Home Office Network Guidance
Remote employees should use a router with WPA2 or WPA3 encryption and change the default router password. Always connect to the company VPN before accessing internal systems from home or public networks.
