# NovaRetail Employee IT Onboarding and Offboarding

## New Hire Setup
New employees receive a laptop, company email account, and identity portal access before their start date. On day one, sign in, set a password of at least 14 characters, and enroll in MFA with the company authenticator app. Standard software is available from the Software Portal. Managers must submit the new hire request at least five business days before the start date.

## Access Requests
Access to shared drives, applications, and cloud resources is requested through the identity portal and approved by the resource owner and the employee's manager. Access follows least privilege and is reviewed every quarter.

## Role Changes and Transfers
When an employee changes teams, the manager submits a role change request. Access tied to the previous role is removed, and new access must be requested separately.

## Offboarding
HR notifies IT of departures. On the last day, IT disables the account, revokes VPN and MFA registrations, and forwards email to the manager for 30 days. Employees must return their laptop, badge, and any hardware security keys. Unreturned equipment is reported to the manager and HR.

## Equipment Refresh
Laptops are replaced every four years. Employees with failing hardware may request an early replacement through the IT Service Desk after diagnostics confirm a fault.
