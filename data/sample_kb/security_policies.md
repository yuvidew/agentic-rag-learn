# NovaRetail Information Security Policies

## Phishing and Suspicious Emails
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. Use the Report Phish button in the email client to report suspicious messages. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, change your password immediately through the self-service portal and contact the IT Service Desk. Security will review the account for unauthorized activity.

## Data Classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer payment data and employee records are Restricted and must only be stored in approved systems. Never store Confidential or Restricted data on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## Removable Media
USB storage devices are blocked by default on company laptops. Requests for an exception require manager approval and IT security review. Approved devices must be encrypted.

## Device Encryption and Screen Lock
All company laptops use full-disk encryption, which must not be disabled. Screens lock automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity. Lock your screen manually whenever you leave your device unattended.

## Reporting a Security Incident
Report suspected security incidents, such as malware alerts, unauthorized access, or data exposure, to the IT Service Desk immediately and mark the ticket P1. Do not delete files or reinstall your system, because this can destroy evidence. Security will coordinate the response.

## PCI Compliance for Store Systems
Point-of-sale systems process cardholder data and are subject to PCI DSS controls. Store staff must not install software on POS terminals, connect personal devices to the POS network, or write down card numbers. Report tampered or missing card readers to Security immediately.
